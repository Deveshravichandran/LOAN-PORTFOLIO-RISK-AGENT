CREDIT MEMO: Acme Corp
Date: Jan 1st, 2025
Industry: Manufacturing
Loan Amount: $10,000,000

Business Overview:
Acme Corp is a legacy manufacturer of heavy machinery. They have stable revenues but are facing pressure from cheaper overseas imports. 

Key Risk Factors:
1. Supply Chain Dependency: Acme relies heavily on steel imports from Taiwan. Any disruption there could severely impact their margins.
2. Labor Relations: They have a unionized workforce. Previous strikes have caused massive revenue hits.
3. Liquidity: They carry a high debt load and need to maintain an Operating Margin above 10% to service interest payments comfortably.

Recommendation:
Approved for $10M facility. Monitor their supply chain costs and any labor disputes closely, as these are the primary drivers of potential default.
